A second home for Haven

Same team. Same community. A new place to train.

Haven Jiu Jitsu has called Baldwinsville home for years. Now we’re bringing Haven to Onondaga Hill with a second location built to make training more accessible for people in Syracuse, near OCC, and on this side of Central New York.
